# Welcome to Tiny Flat Hacks! 🏡

If you’ve ever tried squeezing a proper dining table into a 35-square-metre London flat, wrestled a velvet sofa up three flights of narrow Manchester stairs, or tried drying laundry inside a cosy studio during a wet British winter — **you are in the right place.**

## Our Story

`tinyflathacks.co.uk` was born out of sheer frustration and a desperate need for more floor space. 

As long-time renters in the UK, we’ve lived through our fair share of housing quirks: tiny box rooms, awkward entryway nooks, narrow Victorian hallways, and strict tenancy rules that ban drilling into walls. We quickly realised that most interior design blogs cater to spacious homes with grand staircases and massive back gardens. 

We wanted to build something practical specifically for **UK renters and small flat owners** who need budget-friendly, clever, and damage-free ways to make small spaces work.

## What We Do

We test, research, and review space-saving hacks, compact furniture, and smart storage solutions tailored to UK flat layouts. 

Whether it's finding the perfect fold-away dining table from Furniturebox, setting up a micro-workspace in the corner of your living room, or turning a tiny balcony into a sunny morning coffee spot, we focus on:

* **Rental-Friendly Hacks:** Zero-damage storage, freestanding organisers, and temporary fixes.
* **Compact Furniture Reviews:** Real-world recommendations for narrow and multi-functional items that fit small rooms.
* **UK-Specific Solutions:** Products and retailers with fast UK delivery and accessible prices.

Thanks for stopping by! We hope our daily hacks help you turn your compact flat into a proper home.
