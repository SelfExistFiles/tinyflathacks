### Intro & Before: The Cluttered Nightmare

Right, hands up if your tiny flat's living room has become, shall we say, a *multi-functional* space since the whole working-from-home thing kicked off? If your hand shot up quicker than a squirrel nabbing a dropped chip in Hyde Park, then welcome, friend, you're in good company.

One minute, it was our workspace, laptops precariously balanced on laps, papers spread like a bad game of solitaire, and mugs of lukewarm tea forming a precarious tower. The next, we were trying to unwind, but with the ghost of unanswered emails still looming, it felt impossible to truly switch off.

The sofa was doing double duty as an office chair, the coffee table as a boardroom, and the whole vibe was just a bit… stressful. We’d try to tidy up, but within an hour, it was back to being a chaotic blur. We needed more than just a tidy; we needed a *transformation*. A proper delineation between where the spreadsheets lived and where the Netflix binges happened.
