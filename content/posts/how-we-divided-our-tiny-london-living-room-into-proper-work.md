### Intro & Before: The Cluttered Nightmare

Right, hands up if your living room has slowly, subtly, become… everything? If you’re nodding along from a tiny flat in London (or Manchester, or Bristol – anywhere with bonkers rent and postage stamp-sized rooms, honestly), then you’ll know exactly what I’m on about. Our little two-bed Victorian conversion in South London has its charms – high ceilings, a bay window that lets in *some* light even on the gloomiest British days – but 'spacious' isn't exactly a word I'd use for it. And when both me and my partner, Tom, started working from home more consistently, our already compact living room became an absolute nightmare.

It wasn't just a place to relax anymore; it was our office, our dining room, our gym (for about two weeks, before the yoga mat became a dust magnet), and frankly, a bit of a dumping ground. We’d tried everything short of building a wall – and frankly, our landlord would have had a fit, not to mention getting a wall up our narrow, winding stairwell would have been a proper mission.

Our sofa, a hand-me-down from Tom’s auntie, was comfortable enough but too bulky, taking up far too much precious floor space. We had a rickety old coffee table that was perpetually covered in laptops, charging cables, half-drunk mugs of tea, and the endless detritus of daily life. There was no clear distinction between ‘work mode’ and ‘chill mode’, and it was honestly driving us bonkers. I'd finish my last Zoom call of the day only to find my laptop still staring at me from the sofa, mocking my attempts at relaxation. Tom would be trying to watch telly, but his work notes would be spread out right beside him. It felt like we were constantly 'at work', even when we weren't.

It looked a bit like this, but probably even messier (you're welcome for the stock photo!):

![Before Makeover](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1555041469-a586c61ea9bc?w=800)

We desperately needed to reclaim our evening space, but how do you do that when you’ve got approximately three square metres to play with? We didn’t want to feel like we were living in our office, nor did we want our 'relaxation zone' to be covered in spreadsheets. The dream was to have a clear 'work zone' that could be tidied away at the end of the day, and a separate, inviting 'chill zone' where we could properly unwind. The mission felt impossible, especially on a renter’s budget.

### The Fix & Furniturebox Discoveries

We spent weeks scrolling through Pinterest and various homeware sites, feeling increasingly dejected. Everything stylish looked either too big for our space, or far too expensive for our wallets. Then, one rainy Saturday (because, well, it’s Britain), I stumbled upon Furniturebox.co.uk. I’d seen a few of their ads pop up, and their prices always seemed ridiculously good, so I thought, why not? What immediately caught my eye was their range of compact, modern furniture – and the 'Next Day Free UK Delivery' part was honestly a total game-changer, especially after a previous experience waiting a week for a delivery in the pouring rain.

We decided our main issue was the bulky sofa, so that was our first target. We needed something smaller, but still comfortable enough for proper lounging. And we wanted something a bit chic, to elevate the space. That's when we found the **Santorini Velvet Sofa**. Oh. My. Goodness. It was love at first scroll. The measurements were spot on for our living room – compact enough to free up some vital floor space, but still a proper two-seater. We went for the gorgeous emerald green, because if you're going small, you might as well go bold, right? The velvet felt really luxurious, and it genuinely looked like it cost far more than it did. It instantly became the focal point of what we hoped would be our new 'relaxation zone'.

Next up was the coffee table conundrum. Our old one was just a big, flat surface for clutter. We needed something adaptable. This is where the **Lucia Nest of Tables** came in. Genius, absolute genius! It's a set of two round tables that nest perfectly inside each other. We figured the smaller one could be pulled out when we needed an extra surface – say, for a cuppa and a book, or even as a side table for my laptop when I needed to quickly check something without fully 'setting up shop'. When not in use, it just tucked away neatly, making the room feel less cluttered. This was going to be proper useful for the flexible work zone we envisioned. It’s amazing how much difference that bit of flexibility makes in a small space.

But wait, we needed *another* coffee table, you might ask? Well, we wanted something that offered a bit more surface area when we were fully relaxing, maybe with a takeaway or a board game. Crucially, we also needed some discrete storage. The **Sienna High Gloss Coffee Table** was the answer. We chose the white high gloss because it has this brilliant reflective quality that helps bounce light around the room, making it feel bigger and brighter – a classic tiny flat hack! But the real kicker was the lift-top storage. Perfect for stashing away laptops, chargers, notebooks, and all the general bits and bobs that previously lived on our main coffee table. At the end of the workday, everything gets tidied away into the Sienna table, and *poof*, the work zone is gone!

The delivery itself was a dream. Seriously, next day delivery meant we weren't waiting around for ages, rearranging our schedules. Putting the flat-pack items together is always an Olympic sport in our household, usually ending in a mild domestic and a strong cuppa, but honestly, the Furniturebox instructions were surprisingly clear. A couple of hours, a few wrong screws (my fault, not theirs!), and a fair bit of tutting later, we had it all sorted.

### After: How It Changed Our Daily Life

The change was instantaneous and genuinely transformative.

With the smaller **Santorini Velvet Sofa**, our living room immediately felt more spacious. It created a defined 'relaxation zone' by the bay window. We paired it with a soft rug and some cosy throws, and it became this inviting haven. Now, in the evenings, we collapse onto it, put our feet up, and genuinely switch off. It feels luxurious and properly comfortable, perfect for binge-watching telly or just reading. No laptops allowed!

The **Lucia Nest of Tables** became the lynchpin of our new, flexible 'work zone'. We positioned the larger of the two tables next to a small, wall-mounted desk (which we already had, luckily) that we rarely used before. Now, one table holds my coffee, the other my mousepad, and it gives me just enough surface area to feel like I have a dedicated workspace without it taking over the entire room. If Tom needs to work on something quickly, he can pull out the smaller table and use it as his own temporary spot. When the workday is done, the tables nest back together, instantly tidying up the area. It’s like magic!

And the **Sienna High Gloss Coffee Table**? That became our secret weapon. Positioned in front of the Santorini sofa, it serves as a stylish, contemporary centrepiece. But at 5:30 pm (or whenever we decide to officially clock off!), the laptops, notebooks, and charging cables are all lifted up and neatly tucked away inside. The sleek, glossy surface is easy to wipe clean, and it really does make the room feel brighter and more organised. It’s amazing how much a bit of hidden storage can reduce visual clutter and, in turn, mental clutter.

Now, we have a clear distinction: the sofa and the Sienna table are for relaxing, watching films, having a chat, or just chilling. The wall-mounted desk and the Lucia nest of tables are for work. It’s a simple shift, but it’s had a profound impact on our daily life. Our evenings feel like our own again. We can sit on our beautiful, comfy sofa, put our feet up, and not feel like we’re still in the office. The room just feels… lighter, more intentional, and crucially, like *our home* again, not a shared workspace. It’s amazing how much happier and less stressed we both feel.
