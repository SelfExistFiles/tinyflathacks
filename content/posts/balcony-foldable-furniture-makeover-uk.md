Right, I'm going to show you something properly embarrassing.

I've got a balcony. It's not massive – maybe 2 metres by 3 metres – but it's a balcony. It's outside space. In London. That's basically a luxury.

But for about a year and a half, I completely wasted it.

It was just a dumping ground for old boxes, a broken laundry rack, a couple of dead plants I kept meaning to water (they were beyond saving), and a bike I never rode. I'd look at it and feel guilty. But I never did anything about it.

---

## The Before: A Complete Wasteland

![Messy balcony with clutter and dead plants](/image_516956879205654.webp)

Look, I'm not proud of this photo. But this is what my balcony looked like for way too long.

**The problems:**
- Old boxes that I'd "just put there temporarily" – they stayed for months
- A broken laundry rack that was too annoying to fix
- Dead plants that made the whole space feel sad
- A bike I never rode, taking up half the space
- Dusty floor that I never swept

**Why I put up with it:**
- I thought the balcony was too small for anything useful
- I didn't want to spend money on outdoor stuff that might get ruined by the rain
- I kept telling myself I'd "sort it out when the weather gets better" – I did this for about 18 months

The worst bit? I had outdoor space and I wasn't using it. I'd look at it from my living room and just feel a bit sad about it.

---

## The Turning Point

One sunny day – you know, the kind we get about five times a year – I opened the balcony door and thought, "This is ridiculous. I'm going to sit out here today."

And then I realised I had nowhere to sit. The floor was dusty, there was a broken laundry rack in the way, and I had nothing to actually sit on.

So I ordered a couple of bits online and finally sorted it out.

---

## The After: Actually Usable

![Tidy balcony with foldable furniture and plants](/image_373263414401775.webp)

This is what it looks like now. Same balcony. Same size. Completely different vibe.

**What I changed:**

**1. A foldable bistro set**
This was the big one. I got a foldable bistro table and two chairs. They're lightweight aluminium – they won't rust in the rain.

**When it's set up:**
- Seats two people comfortably
- Perfect for morning coffee or an evening drink
- Makes the space feel intentional

**When it's packed away:**
- Folds completely flat
- Lives behind the sofa (takes up no floor space)
- I bring it out when the weather's decent

**2. A foldable plant stand**
I got a little plant stand with three tiers. It folds flat when I don't need it.

**What's on it now:**
- A couple of hardy plants that survive outside
- Some herbs – basil and mint (which I actually use)
- A small lantern for when it's warm

**3. A foldable clothes airer**
I swapped the broken laundry rack for a foldable one. It works properly, folds flat, and lives behind the door when I'm not using it.

## What Actually Made the Biggest Difference

Honestly, it was just having somewhere to sit.

Before, I'd open the balcony door, see the mess, and just close it again. Now I've got a proper setup, I actually go out there. Morning coffee, reading a book, even a bit of work sometimes.

It's made my flat feel bigger, because it's like I've got an extra room. A small, outdoorsy room that I can use when the weather's decent.

---

## What I'd Do Differently

If I did this again:

**1. I'd get a bigger bistro set.**
The one I got is fine – seats two, foldable, works. But I could have gone for one with a slightly larger table surface. It's a bit too small for a laptop if I want to work out here.

**2. I'd get a better plant stand.**
The one I got is foldable and nice, but it's a bit flimsy. I'd spend a bit more on something sturdier next time.

**3. I'd do it sooner.**
This is the main one. I wasted about 18 months not using my balcony. I could have been sitting out here all that time. Don't do what I did.
