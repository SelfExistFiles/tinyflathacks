Right, if you're anything like us, living in a flat in a bustling UK city – let's say, somewhere with tight staircases and a healthy dose of grey skies, like our little place in Manchester – you'll know the struggle is real. More than once, we've had to call in reinforcements (usually my dad, who's a wizard with an Allen key) to sort us out. The thought of adding more assembly woes to our lives just wasn’t appealing.
