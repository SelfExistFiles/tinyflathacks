Right, I'm going to show you something that's been bothering me for ages.

Every flat has one. That awkward corner that's just there. Too small for a sofa, too weird for a proper table, so it just ends up being a dumping ground for stuff you don't know where to put.

This was mine. For about two years, it was just a pile of junk. I hated it, but I kept ignoring it because I didn't know what else to do.

---

## The Before: A Proper State

![Messy corner with clutter and random items piled up](/image_335680450030611.webp)

Look, I'm not proud of this. But this is what my living room corner looked like for way too long.

**The problems:**
- Random boxes I never unpacked from the last move
- A stack of books I'd read but couldn't be bothered to shelf
- A folding chair that was broken and I kept meaning to fix (never did)
- A bag of cables and chargers – you know, the universal "I might need this one day" pile
- Dust. So much dust.

**Why I put up with it:**
- I thought the corner was too small for anything useful
- I didn't want to spend money on something that might not fit
- I kept telling myself I'd "sort it out" – classic avoidance behaviour

The worst bit? This corner is right next to my sofa. Everyone who came over could see it. Proper embarrassing.

---

## The Turning Point

One weekend I had a mate over who'd just got into interior stuff – you know the type, all mood boards and colour palettes. She looked at my corner and just went, "Why don't you just put something there?"

And I was like, "It's too small for anything."

And she was like, "Have you actually measured it?"

I hadn't. I'd just assumed.

So I measured it. And it was 85cm wide. Not tiny. Not huge. But definitely big enough for something if I was smart about it.

That's when I started looking at foldable furniture.

---

## The After: Actually Useful

![Tidy corner with a foldable desk and storage ottoman](/image_814939096149826.webp)

This is what it looks like now. Same corner. Same flat. Completely different vibe.

**What I changed:**

**1. A foldable corner desk**
I found this foldable desk that's designed specifically for corners – it's triangular, fits into the space perfectly, and has a drop-leaf that folds up when I need more surface.

**What I use it for:**
- Laptop when I'm working from home (instead of the kitchen table)
- A place to put my coffee when I'm reading
- A little plant corner – I've got two small ones on it now

When I don't need it, the leaf folds down and it takes up basically no space.

**2. A storage ottoman on wheels**
I got a small storage ottoman that fits right next to the desk. It's on wheels, so I can move it around if I need extra seating.

**Inside it:**
- Those random cables and chargers (finally organised in a box)
- Spare blankets
- A couple of board games

**3. A foldable reading lamp**
This is a small thing, but it's made a difference. I got a foldable floor lamp with a flexible neck. When I'm reading, I pull it out. When I'm not, I fold it flat and tuck it behind the ottoman.

## What Actually Made the Biggest Difference

Honestly, it was just having a proper *purpose* for the space.

Before, it was just "the corner" – no function, no plan, just stuff. Now it's my little work/reading nook. I actually sit there when I'm on the phone or just want to look out the window.

The foldable stuff works because it's not permanent. I can change it up, move it around, or pack it away if I need the space.

---

## What I'd Do Differently

If I did this again:

**1. I'd measure properly before buying anything.**
I got lucky – the desk fit perfectly. But I was a few centimetres off it being a disaster. Measure. Twice.

**2. I'd get a desk with a bit more storage.**
The one I got folds up nicely, but there's no drawer or shelf underneath. I've got a couple of books that just sit on the surface now – not a big deal, but I'd probably get one with a little shelf if I did it again.

**3. I'd buy the ottoman first.**
I bought the desk first, then realised the ottoman would fit perfectly next to it. If I'd planned better, I'd have got them together.
