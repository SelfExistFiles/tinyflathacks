If you live in a small flat, your bed takes up a huge chunk of floor space. Why not make that space work harder? A **storage bed** is one of the smartest investments you can make for a compact bedroom.

Here are 3 of the best storage bed styles for UK flats, each solving a different space problem.

---

### 1. Ottoman Bed – Maximum Hidden Storage
An ottoman bed lifts up to reveal a massive storage area underneath – perfect for suitcases, winter duvets, and out-of-season clothes.

**Why it works:** Holds more than any drawer system.  
**Best for:** Flats with limited wardrobe space.

---

### 2. Drawer Storage Bed – Easy Daily Access
Drawer beds have 2–4 pull-out drawers built into the base. Great for everyday items like bedding, pyjamas, or jumpers.

**Why it works:** No need to lift the mattress – just slide and grab.  
**Best for:** Anyone who wants quick, organised access.

---

### 3. Divan Storage Bed – Classic & Customisable
Divan beds are a UK favourite. They come with a sturdy base, often with built-in drawers, and you can choose your own headboard to match your style.

**Why it works:** Affordable, durable, and works with any room layout.  
**Best for:** Traditional flats or rented rooms.
