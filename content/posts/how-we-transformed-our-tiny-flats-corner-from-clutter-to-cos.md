Honestly, you wouldn't believe it. This corner, nestled between the kitchen entrance and the living room proper, was a multi-functional monstrosity. We had this rickety old square table we'd inherited from a previous flatmate – bless its heart, it tried – but it was just too big for the space and too small for actual guests. It was neither fish nor fowl, just a bloody nuisance.

It made the whole flat feel cramped and chaotic. We'd always wanted a proper space to eat a meal, maybe even host a couple of mates without feeling like we were playing Tetris with the furniture. But with such a tight footprint, it felt like an impossible dream. Every time we looked at it, we’d sigh, shrug, and then pile another coat on top. It was a proper nightmare, I tell you.
