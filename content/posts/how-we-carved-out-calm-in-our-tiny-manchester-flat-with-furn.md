Right, let’s be honest. Living in a flat in Manchester, especially one that’s not exactly sprawling, comes with its fair share of… *compromises*. Our little two-bed has been home for a good few years now, and while I absolutely adore the vibe of the city, the reality of tiny flat living had started to really wear me down, particularly in the living room.

### Before: The Cluttered Nightmare

Our 'living room' was, to be frank, a bit of a disaster zone. It wasn't just where we watched telly; it was also my full-time office, our dining area (if you count eating off your lap), and the dumping ground for anything that didn't have a designated home – which, let's face it, was most things.

![Before Makeover](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1555041469-a586c61ea9bc?w=800)

We had this bulky, hand-me-down sofa that swallowed up half the room and looked perpetually tired, no matter how much we plumped its cushions. Opposite it, a rickety old desk, laden with my laptop, monitor, and enough cables to rival a spaghetti junction. There was no real coffee table, just a couple of mismatched footstools that usually ended up covered in mugs, remote controls, and unopened mail.

The result? Mental clutter. It felt like I was constantly living in my office, even when I was trying to relax. My brain just couldn’t switch off. I'd finish work, look up, and still be staring at the remnants of my workday, the lines between 'work' and 'home' blurring into one big, overwhelming mess. The idea of having mates over was a bloody nightmare; I'd spend half an hour frantically shoving paperwork into drawers, pretending it was always this tidy. Something had to give. We desperately needed to carve out some proper zones.
