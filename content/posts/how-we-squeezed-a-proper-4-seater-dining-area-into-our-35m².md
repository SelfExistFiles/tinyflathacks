Right, let’s be entirely honest about living in a 35m² Victorian conversion flat in South London. It looks lovely on Instagram with the high ceilings and original floorboards, but in reality, space is at an absolute premium. 

For the past fourteen months, my partner and I have been living the classic British renter lifestyle. That meant eating curry off our knees on the sofa, balancing hot plates on sofa arms, and navigating around a giant heated clothes airer that permanently lives in the middle of the room because, well, it’s Britain and it rains four days out of seven.

Our living room had to do everything: function as a home office, a lounge, a dry-laundry hub, and somehow a dining room. We had this awkward, dark corner next to the window measuring roughly 1.6 metres by 1.4 metres. It had become a dumping ground for unopened post, spare tote bags, and a dodgy secondhand desk I bought off Gumtree in 2022. 

We desperately wanted a proper place to sit down, eat a meal like actual grown-ups, and maybe even host two friends for dinner without making them sit on the edge of our bed. But finding furniture that fits a poky UK flat without making the place feel like an obstacle course felt like a bit of a nightmare.

### My Top 3 Tips for Renovating a Small Flat Corner:

1. **Measure three times, order once:** Use masking tape on your carpet or laminate to map out the *exact* footprint of the furniture when extended. Walk around it. Make sure you can still open nearby doors.
2. **Go light and leggy:** Heavy, dark wooden furniture with solid bases will make a small flat feel like a cave. Go for gloss, glass, or light tones, and pick chairs with thin, metal, or tapered legs that let light pass through.
3. **Invest in extendable pieces:** If you only need two seats 80% of the time, don't buy a massive 4-seater that permanently steals your floor space. Get a folding or extendable table that adapts when you actually have people over.
