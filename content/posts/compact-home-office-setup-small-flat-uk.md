Working from home is here to stay, but not all of us have a spare room for a dedicated office. The good news? You can create a comfortable, productive workspace in almost any corner of your flat.

Here’s how to build a **compact home office** that actually works.

---

## 1. Pick the Right Spot
Find a corner, a wall, or even a large wardrobe gap. Your workspace doesn't need much – just enough room for a desk, a chair, and a power socket.

- **Avoid the sofa:** It’s too comfortable and bad for your back.
- **Face a window if possible:** Natural light boosts mood and focus.

---

## 2. Choose a Small-Footprint Desk
Wall-mounted desks, folding desks, and slim console-style desks are all great for small flats.

> 💡 **Pro Tip:** A drop-leaf desk can be folded down when not in use – perfect for studio flats.

[Browse Small Desks](https://www.furniturebox.co.uk/desks)
---

## 3. Get the Right Chair
You don’t need a bulky office chair. Look for a compact, ergonomic chair that tucks under the desk when not in use.

---

## 4. Keep Storage Simple
Add a small shelf or a wall-mounted organiser above your desk. This keeps your workspace tidy without using up precious floor space.

---

## 💎 Quick Checklist

| Item | Why It's Essential |
|------|---------------------|
| Compact desk | Fits in any room |
| Ergonomic chair | Protects your back |
| Good lighting | Reduces eye strain |
| Cable tidy | Keeps it neat |

Your home office doesn't have to be big. It just has to work for you. A few thoughtful choices can turn any corner into a productive space.
