For ages, our living room was, to put it mildly, a bit of a chaotic mess. Picture this: a sofa (too big, inherited, and frankly, a bit lumpy), a rather wobbly coffee table, and then, plonked right in the middle of it all, my partner’s makeshift 'office'.

He’s been working from home since, well, you know, that whole thing. And our living room, bless its cotton socks, was pulling double duty. Triple duty, really. It was our Netflix binge zone, our dinner-on-the-sofa spot, and his daily grind headquarters.
