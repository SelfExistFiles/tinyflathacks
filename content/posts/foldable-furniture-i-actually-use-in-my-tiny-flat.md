This isn't a list of fancy designer pieces. This is the stuff I actually bought, kept, and use every week.

## What I'd Actually Say to Someone Considering Foldable Stuff

Honestly, go for it – but manage your expectations.

Foldable furniture is a trade-off. It's not as solid or permanent as proper furniture. If you want stuff that'll last 50 years and survive your grandchildren, this isn't it.

**But if you live in a small flat and you're sick of not having space?** Foldable stuff is genuinely useful.

**What I learned:**
- Buy for your *actual* life, not the life you wish you had
- Don't go for the cheapest option – the mechanism matters
- Measure twice before ordering (I've made this mistake, it's annoying)

The ones that lasted for me are the ones where I spent a bit more – like the Furniturebox drop-leaf desk, which has proper metal hinges and a leg that locks properly.
