Right, let's be honest. Living in a small flat in a buzzing city like Manchester (or London, or Bristol – you know the drill) comes with its own unique set of challenges. ### The Cluttered Nightmare: Before the Makeover

For ages, our living room corner served a multitude of purposes, none of them particularly well. It was the dumping ground for coats, the temporary home for newly delivered parcels, and, most frequently, the precarious spot where we attempted to eat our dinner. You know the drill: plates balanced on knees, a remote control perilously close to your gravy, and the constant fear of knocking over a drink. Honestly, it was a bloody nightmare.

![Before Makeover](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1555041469-a586c61ea9bc?w=800)

We’d tried everything. A tiny folding table that felt like it belonged in a child’s playhouse. A couple of wobbly stools that made you feel like you were about to topple over with every mouthful. Nothing quite worked. The space was just... awkward. Too big for nothing, too small for everything. It was a wasted corner, a constant reminder of our small-space struggles.

We often talked about having friends over for a proper meal, but the thought of trying to squeeze everyone around our makeshift 'dining' setup was enough to make us sweat. We’d end up ordering a takeaway and sprawling on the sofa, which, while cosy, wasn't exactly the sophisticated dinner party vibe we sometimes craved. Plus, I was getting seriously fed up with the perpetual crumbs in the sofa cushions. It was time for a proper intervention.
