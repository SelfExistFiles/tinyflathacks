### Intro & Before: The Cluttered Nightmare

Both of us were crammed in there, one perched precariously on a dining chair dragged in from the kitchen, the other attempting to type with a laptop balanced on a cushion on the sofa.

It was, to put it mildly, a nightmare. Our small, L-shaped sofa (a hand-me-down that had seen better days, bless its saggy cushions) was covered in a permanent layer of work paraphernalia – notebooks, chargers, half-empty mugs of tea. We’d try to tidy it away each evening, but honestly, who has the energy after a full day of staring at a screen? The whole room just screamed "chaos," and it was making us feel utterly exhausted and mentally jumbled. There was no clear divide, no "off" switch. It was just one big, grey, muddled blob of living.

We desperately needed to carve out some proper zones: a dedicated space for work that felt productive, and a genuinely relaxing area where we could properly switch off. But how do you do that in a room that’s barely bigger than a postage stamp?
