Right, let's be honest, living in a tiny flat in the UK, especially in a city like Manchester, comes with its own particular set of challenges. One minute you're trying to enjoy a cosy night in, the next you're tripping over a pile of work documents that have somehow migrated from the kitchen counter to the living room floor. Sound familiar? That was pretty much our daily existence in our little two-bed conversion flat in Chorlton.

### The Cluttered Nightmare: Before Our Great Divide

Our living room, bless its cotton socks, had to do *everything*. It was our dining room, our chill-out zone, our cinema (on a projector, naturally, because who has space for a massive TV?), and, increasingly, our makeshift office. My partner works from home full-time, and I often have bits and bobs to do in the evenings. This meant our one sofa was constantly littered with laptops, chargers, notebooks, half-drunk mugs of tea, and, more often than not, a rogue dinner plate.

Honestly, it was a proper nightmare. We'd finish work for the day, only to realise we were still sitting amongst the tools of our labour. There was no real 'off switch'. The mental load of seeing your workspace constantly encroaching on your relaxation space is no joke, especially when another rainy Manchester day means you're stuck indoors with it all. Our small space felt even smaller, and we were constantly feeling a bit frazzled. We needed a proper solution, and fast.
