Alright, let's be honest, living in a flat in the UK can be a proper squeeze. Especially when you're in a city like Manchester, where every square foot comes with a hefty price tag. ### The Cluttered Nightmare Before

Picture this: our 'living room' was essentially one room trying to do the job of three. It was our dining room, our chill-out zone, and, increasingly, our makeshift office. My laptop was perpetually perched on the arm of the old, lumpy sofa, surrounded by coffee cups, important-looking documents (that usually turned out to be flyers), and whatever snack happened to be within arm's reach. My partner's setup wasn't much better, usually involving balancing his screen on a stack of books on a wobbly stool.

By 5 pm, the lines were irrevocably blurred. The laptop was still there, buzzing away, a silent reminder of emails still to answer, even as we tried to unwind with a cuppa. The space never felt like *ours* to relax in, and honestly, the constant visual clutter was doing a number on our mental state. It was a proper nightmare.

We’d often try to make excuses for the mess, muttering about "cosy living" and "multi-functional spaces", but deep down, we knew it was just an unorganised disaster. The small size of our flat felt like a curse, forcing us into this messy co-existence of work and play, with neither getting the dedicated space it truly deserved.
