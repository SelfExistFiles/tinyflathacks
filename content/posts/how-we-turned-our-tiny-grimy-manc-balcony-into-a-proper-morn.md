It was less a space for enjoyment and more an outdoor junk drawer. Every time I looked out there, especially on one of Manchester's famously grey mornings, my heart would sink a little. The dream of sipping a proper brew outside, feeling a bit of fresh (or at least, less-flat-smelling) air, seemed utterly ridiculous.
