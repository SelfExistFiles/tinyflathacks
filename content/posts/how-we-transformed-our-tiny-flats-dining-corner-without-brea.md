Right, let's be honest. Living in a dinky two-bed flat in Bristol, space is always the absolute priority. Every square inch counts, especially when you’re trying to squeeze in all the essentials and still have room to, you know, actually *live*. For ages, our 'dining area' was, honestly, a bit of a laugh. More of a multi-purpose dumping ground, really.

Dinner was usually eaten on the sofa, balanced precariously on laps, or standing up at the kitchen counter. Trying to host more than one person? Forget about it. We knew we needed to make a change, but the thought of trying to fit a standard dining table into our already packed flat gave us the shivers. Plus, we’re renting, so no major structural changes allowed, and our budget isn't exactly limitless. We needed something clever, something that could adapt to our tiny space, and most importantly, something that wouldn't require a second mortgage.
