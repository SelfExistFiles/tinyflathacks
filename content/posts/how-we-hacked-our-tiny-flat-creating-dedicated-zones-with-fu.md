### Intro & Before: The Cluttered Nightmare

Right, hands up if you live in a flat that feels like it’s constantly trying to swallow you whole? Yeah, thought so. I’m writing this from our cosy (read: bloody tiny) one-bed in Manchester, and honestly, for ages, our living room was a proper disaster zone.

Picture this: my partner and I both work from home a couple of days a week, and our living room was trying its best to be *everything*. It was the office, the dining room, the chill-out zone, and the place where our ever-growing mountain of Amazon boxes lived. The sofa was constantly covered in laptops, charging cables, half-eaten lunch plates, and a rogue sock or two. There was no real distinction between ‘work mode’ and ‘unwind mode’. We’d finish a call, shut the laptop, and it was still *right there*, staring at us from the coffee table. The whole thing was a bit of a nightmare, blurring the lines between work and relaxation, making it impossible to switch off.

![Before Makeover](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1555041469-a586c61ea9bc?w=800)

Honestly, by 5 pm most days, the room felt less like a sanctuary and more like a cluttered battleground. We’d be tripping over each other, trying to find space for our evening cuppa amongst the paperwork. It was stressing us out and making our already small flat feel even smaller. We needed a proper solution, something that would help us mentally *and* physically divide our space, without having to knock down any walls (a dream, but highly unlikely in a rental, eh?).

### The Fix & Furniturebox Discoveries

Because, let's be real, London (or Manchester, or Bristol for that matter!) rents are enough to make your eyes water without adding extortionate furniture prices on top.
